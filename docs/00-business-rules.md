> Hollow Creek Poultry Co. is a fictional company. All policies, prices, locations
> and people here are synthetic, created for a portfolio project.

These are the approved HR rules the Roost assistant follows and the handbook is
written from. Every requirement, test and eval in this project traces back to
this document.

## 1. Organization

The company has three processing plants and one corporate office, each with its
own HR team. There is no union.

| Location | HR team | Who works there |
|---|---|---|
| Georgia Plant | Georgia Plant HR | Weekly Production, Drivers, Weekly IT Help Desk |
| Arkansas Plant | Arkansas Plant HR | Weekly Production, Drivers, Weekly IT Help Desk |
| North Carolina Plant | North Carolina Plant HR | Weekly Production, Drivers, Weekly IT Help Desk |
| Corporate Office (Texas) | Corporate HR | Executive, Biweekly, Weekly IT Help Desk |

Every driver is assigned to one plant. IT Help Desk staff belong to the HR team
where they are based.

## 2. Payroll classes

Executive and Biweekly are salaried and overtime-exempt; all three weekly classes
are hourly and paid 1.5x over 40 hours a week.

| Class | Who | Pay frequency | Overtime | Schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | VP, Director, CEO | Every 2 weeks (26 paychecks/yr) | Exempt | 8–5, Mon–Fri |
| Biweekly | Salary over $75k, not an executive; all at corporate | Every 2 weeks (26 paychecks/yr) | Exempt | 8–5, Mon–Fri |
| Weekly IT Help Desk | All other IT support staff | Weekly | 1.5x over 40 hrs/week | 8–5, Mon–Fri |
| Weekly Production | Plant floor and sanitation | Weekly | 1.5x over 40 hrs/week | Shifts |

**Paydays.** Weekly classes are paid every Friday for the prior Sunday–Saturday
week. Executive and Biweekly are paid every other Friday.

**Shifts and routes.** All production shifts are fixed, Monday to Friday with

## 3. Vacation (PTO)

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | 10 days/year for every class, tracked in hours (10 × scheduled shift length: 80 hrs for most, 100 hrs for drivers) |
| Accrual | Earned every pay period |
| New hires | Accrue from day 1; usable after 90 days |
| Smallest request | Half day |
| Notice | At least 14 days. With less notice, the assistant does not submit and tells the employee to talk to their supervisor. |
| Most days in a row | 10 working days; longer stretches need HR |
| Blackout | Nov 15 – Dec 31 for Weekly Production |
| Department limit | At most 15% of one department, at one location, on one shift, off on the same day (departments span plants) |
| Carryover | Up to 5 unused days carry over; the rest expire Dec 31 |

## 4. Vacation approval flow

No request reaches an approver until it passes the rules and the employee
confirms it; then it needs two approvals.

1. The employee asks for time off in Teams or at a kiosk.
2. The assistant checks the rules: notice, 90-day wait, blackout, department
   limit, days in a row, and balance (silently).
3. If any rule fails, the request is not submitted. The assistant explains why
   and refers the employee to their supervisor or HR.
4. If all rules pass, the assistant shows a summary and the employee confirms.
5. The supervisor or manager approves or denies from a Teams message.
6. HR approves or denies in Teams (plant HR, or Corporate HR for corporate staff).
7. The employee is notified by Teams or email; drivers check with plant HR.
8. If there is no decision within 3 business days, or 2 days before the start
   date (whichever comes first), the employee is told to check with HR in person.
   There is no automatic escalation.

- **Approvers.** Plant employees: supervisor or manager, then plant HR. Corporate
  staff: supervisor, then Corporate HR. The CEO notifies Corporate HR; no
  approval is needed.
- **Balance check.** Done silently. If the request is more than the employee has,
  the assistant says so and refers them to HR, without showing a number.
- **Department limit.** If 15% of the employee's department at their location
  and on their shift is already off, the assistant suggests the nearest
  available dates.
- **Denials.** The employee is notified the same way as for approvals.
- **Changes.** Editing a request, even an approved one, restarts approval.
  Cancelling before the start date just notifies the approvers.

## 6. Attendance points

Weekly Production and Drivers are on a points system, with a termination review
at 10 points in 12 months.

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| No-call/no-show | 3 (three in a row counts as job abandonment) |
| Absent, called the attendance line less than 1 hour before shift | 1.5 |
| Absent, called at least 1 hour before shift | 1 |
| Late or leaving early, under 2 hours | 0.5 |
| Approved PTO, sick, bereavement, jury duty, FMLA, workers' comp, holidays | 0 |

- Points expire 12 months after they are given.
- 90 days of perfect attendance removes 1 point.
- Discipline: verbal warning at 5 points, written warning at 7, final warning at
  9, termination review at 10.
- The assistant explains the policy but never shows anyone's point total.

## 8. What the assistant can and cannot do

**Can do**

- Answer policy questions, citing the handbook section and its effective date
- Answer payroll schedule questions, such as paydays and pay periods
- Create, change and cancel vacation requests after checking the rules

**Never does**

- Show pay, vacation, sick or attendance-point balances, or any financial or bank
  data. These questions go to HR.
- Arrange shift swaps (it tells the employee to check with their manager)
- Decide eligibility or give legal advice

**Sensitive matters.** Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, injury, safety
hazards, medical or accommodation needs, immigration status, wage disputes and
personal crises get the employee's HR contact and the ethics hotline.

- In an emergency, the assistant gives 911 and plant safety contacts first.
- For HR topics, it offers to send HR a short summary, but only with the
  employee's permission.
- Ethics hotline matters are never forwarded, so they stay anonymous.

## 9. Channels, language and records

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Teams | Sign-in through the employee's Microsoft account. Language detected from what they type; they can switch anytime. |
| Kiosk | Employee number + 6-digit PIN, hardened (decision 6). Logs out after 2 minutes idle. Language buttons on the start screen (EN / ES / VI). |
| Accounts | Everyone except drivers has email or Teams. Drivers use the kiosk and visit plant HR in person. |
| Handbook language | English is the binding version. Spanish and Vietnamese answers say they are translations. |
| Disclaimer | A short footer on every reply: informational only, HR's decision is final |
| Chat logs | Kept 45 days unless under legal hold (decision 7), visible only to HR admins |
| Audit trail | Requests and approvals kept permanently |
| Handbook updates | HR uploads new versions |
